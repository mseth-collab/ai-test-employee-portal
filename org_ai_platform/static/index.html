What each department serves

HR

Top areas

  • Leave & attendance — Apply, balance, policy by region
  • Policies & handbook — Code of conduct, remote work, leave types
  • Benefits — Insurance, wellness, allowances
  • Payroll & tax — Slips, deductions, year-end
  • Onboarding & offboarding — Checklists, assets, access
  • Org structure & directory — Who reports where, contact info
  • Training & learning — Mandatory courses, certifications

Key inputs we need

  • Employee ID, request type, dates (for leave), policy reference, region/country
Finance

Top areas

  • Expenses & reimbursements — Submit, approve, status
  • Travel & T&E — Claims, per diem, policy
  • Invoices & vendor payments — PO, payment status
  • Budgets & cost centers — View, allocate, approvals
  • Approvals — Delegation, workflow, escalation
  • Audit & compliance — Policy questions, audit trail

Key inputs we need

  • Employee ID, cost center, amount, currency, receipt/attachment, approval level, region
Operations

Top areas

  • Facilities — Desk, meeting rooms, site access
  • Travel — Bookings, policy, visas
  • Procurement — Purchase requests, vendors, thresholds
  • Vendor management — Onboarding, contracts, payments
  • Assets — Laptop, phone, return, damage
  • Logistics & shipping — Courier, samples, equipment

Key inputs we need

  • Employee ID, site/region, asset type, dates, vendor name, value, justification
Tech

Top areas

  • IT helpdesk — Password reset, MFA, login issues
  • Access requests — Apps, folders, VPN, SSO
  • Software & hardware — Install, license, device
  • Tickets — Log, track, escalate
  • Outage & status — Service health, ETA
  • Security — Phishing report, policy, training

Key inputs we need

  • Employee ID, ticket type, app/system name, description, urgency, region/timezone
Grievance

Top areas

  • Raise a complaint — Harassment, discrimination, ethics, safety
  • Check status — Case number, next steps
  • Anonymous reporting — Hotline, channel, non-retaliation
  • Policy — Code of conduct, escalation, timelines
  • Escalation — When and how to escalate

Key inputs we need

  • Optional anonymity, category of concern, description, any evidence (optional), region for policy
